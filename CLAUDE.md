# Zhiyan Automation — working rules

## Email: never mix businesses
- All Zhiyan Automation email (outreach, follow-ups, replies) is sent ONLY from
  **sales@zhiyanautomation.com** via the Zoho Mail connection.
- **Never** use the Gmail connector / admin@baraqapropertyservices.com for anything
  Zhiyan-related. Baraqa is a separate, unrelated business.
- Before sending any email, confirm which account/connection it will be sent from.

## Prospect lists
- `prospects/*_prospects.csv` hold company-level contacts only (published general
  inboxes such as info@/sales@). Do not collect personal emails of individuals.
- Every outreach email must identify Zhiyan, be relevant to the recipient's business,
  and include an opt-out line; honour "no thanks" by marking Status "Do not contact".
